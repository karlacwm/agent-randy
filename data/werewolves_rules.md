# Werewolves of Miller's Hollow: Official Rules

Source: https://www.zygomatic-games.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/werewolvesofmillershollow_en_rules_compressed.pdf

## Objective
- **Villagers (and special roles):** Win by eliminating all Werewolves.
- **Werewolves:** Win by eliminating all Villagers.

## Setup
Players sit in a circle. Each player is secretly dealt one character card. The Moderator (or app) directs the flow of the game, calling the phases.

## Defined Role Cards (Official Core Set in this project)
- Villager
- Werewolf
- Cupid
- Seer
- Witch
- Little Girl
- Hunter
- Mayor

## Moderator Responsibilities
- Keep strict action order at night.
- Ensure eliminated players remain silent.
- Resolve simultaneous effects consistently (for example Lover chain reactions).

## Phase 1: The Night
During the Night phase, everyone closes their eyes. The Moderator calls upon specific characters to wake up one by one in a strict order to perform their actions.

### Order of Awakening
1. **Cupid (First night only):** Wakes up and points to two players to be "Lovers". Cupid goes to sleep. The Moderator taps the two lovers. They wake up and acknowledge each other, then go to sleep. (If one lover dies, the other immediately dies of a broken heart).
2. **The Seer:** Wakes up and points to one player. The Moderator silently shows the Seer that player's secret card. The Seer goes to sleep.
3. **The Werewolves:** Wake up together. They silently agree on one victim to kill. They go to sleep.
4. **The Witch:** Wakes up. The Moderator points to the victim chosen by the Werewolves. The Witch can choose to use her Healing Potion to save the victim, use her Poison Potion to kill another player, do both, or do nothing. She has only ONE of each potion for the entire game. The Witch goes to sleep.
5. **The Little Girl (Passive):** Does not get called by the Moderator. The Little Girl is allowed to secretly peek through her fingers *only* while the Werewolves are awake. If the Werewolves catch her peeking, she is immediately killed instead of their original victim.

## Phase 2: The Day
1. Everyone opens their eyes.
2. The Moderator announces who was killed during the night.
3. **The Hunter (Reaction):** If the Hunter is killed (either at night or voted out during the day), they immediately reveal their card and MUST fire their gun, choosing one other player to die instantly with them.
4. **Discussion:** The surviving players debate who the Werewolves might be.
5. **Voting:** The village votes on who to execute. The player with the majority of votes is executed, reveals their card, and is eliminated.

## Special Rules & Edge Cases
- **The Mayor:** Elected by the village during the first day. The Mayor's vote counts as TWO votes. If the Mayor is killed, they choose their successor with their dying breath.
- **Witch Potions:** The Witch CAN use the healing potion on herself. She can use both potions in the same night.
- **Dead Players:** Eliminated players must not speak, gesture, or participate in the game in any way.

## Timing Clarifications
- Night powers resolve in moderator-defined role order.
- Hunter reaction is immediate when Hunter dies.
- Lover death chain resolves immediately after a Lover dies.

## Notes for This Project
- This project applies official-rules-first behavior from the referenced source.
- If your group uses role variants or custom ordering, include that in your prompt.
